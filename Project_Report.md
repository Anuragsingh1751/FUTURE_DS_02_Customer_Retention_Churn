# Telco Customer Retention & Churn — Project Report

## Executive Summary
The analysis examines 7,043 telecom customers and identifies patterns associated with customer churn.

## KPI Summary
- Customers: 7,043
- Churned: 1,869
- Retained: 5,174
- Churn Rate: 26.54%

## Major Findings
1. Month-to-month customers have substantially higher churn than customers on one-year or two-year contracts.
2. Customers in their first 12 months represent an important retention opportunity.
3. Fiber optic customers show higher churn than DSL and customers without internet service.
4. Retained customers have a longer average tenure than churned customers.
5. Combining contract, tenure, and internet-service characteristics helps identify higher-risk groups.

## Recommendations
- Improve onboarding during the first year.
- Encourage longer-term contracts with suitable offers.
- Proactively engage high-risk customers before renewal or cancellation.
- Investigate service experience among Fiber optic customers.
- Use customer segments for targeted retention campaigns.
